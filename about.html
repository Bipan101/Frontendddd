WHY THIRD EYE

From footage
to grounded evidence.

A focused review tool for possible road accidents and fires. It combines fast frame screening with a second, evidence-led visual check.

01 / THE PRINCIPLE

Technology can flag a moment. Evidence decides what it means.

THE WORKFLOW

Three steps. One clear record.

01

Screen

OpenCV samples the video. A YOLO classifier identifies frames that may show fire or a road accident.

YOLO · 4 FRAMES / SECOND
02

Verify

Gemma reviews candidate frames and looks for visible evidence. Ambiguous scenes should not be treated as confirmed incidents.

OLLAMA · GEMMA 3
03

Record

Confirmed snapshots are collected into a PDF report with visible evidence, confidence, and recommended actions.
